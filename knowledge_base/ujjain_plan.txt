Title:🌟 My Ujjain Plan: Jyotirlinga Vibes + Spiritual Power! 🙏🔥

Jai Mahakaal! 🔱🔥
Ujjain — the ancient city where time feels slower, the air feels sacred, and every corner vibrates with the power of Lord Shiva.
It’s the city of Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, deep traditions, and a spiritual energy that hits straight to the soul.

Let’s explore Ujjain in full Mahakaal style! 😌✨

🔱 1. Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga Temple – Soul of Ujjain

The beating spiritual heart of Ujjain!
One of the 12 Jyotirlingas, this temple is pure power.
The famous Bhasma Aarti at 4 AM is a once-in-a-lifetime divine moment — goosebumps guaranteed! 🔥

🕰️ Timings: 3:00 AM – 11:00 PM
💸 Entry: Free
⭐ Bhasma Aarti Ticket: ₹200 (online booking recommended)
📍 Location: Jaisinghpura, Ujjain

🎯 Tips:

Book Bhasma Aarti a day before — seats fill fast!

No mobiles inside the garbhagriha.

Reach early morning for peaceful darshan.

⚔️ 2. Kal Bhairav Temple – Guardian of Ujjain

Dedicated to Lord Kal Bhairav, the fierce protector of the city.
Here, devotees offer liquor as prasad — a unique ritual seen nowhere else!

🕰️ Timings: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
📍 Location: Bhairavgarh Road
💸 Entry: Free

🎯 Tip:
Buy the liquor offering from official counters only (₹100–₹200).

🌅 3. Ram Ghat – Shipra River Aarti

The most peaceful and sacred ghat of Ujjain.
Sit by the water, enjoy the breeze, and feel the devotion in the air.
The evening aarti is magical — diyas glowing on the Shipra River. ✨

🕰️ Best Time: Sunrise & sunset
📍 Location: Near Harsiddhi Temple

🎯 Tip: Take a slow boat ride during sunset — unforgettable! 🛶

🌺 4. Harsiddhi Mata Temple

One of the Shaktipeeths of India.
Famous for its twin deep-stambhs (lamp towers) that light up beautifully in the evening.
A must-visit for divine feminine energy! 💖

🕰️ Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
📍 Location: Near Ram Ghat
💸 Entry: Free

🎯 Tip: Visit during Navratri — the view is breathtaking! 🌸💫

🌌 5. Mangalnath Temple – Planet Mars Temple

Known as the birthplace of Mangal (Mars) according to Hindu astrology.
The view from the temple is peaceful and calming. 🌄

🕰️ Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
📍 Location: Mangalnath Road
💸 Entry: Free

🎯 Tip: Visit in the morning for meditation & peace.

📚 6. Sandipani Ashram – Krishna’s Gurukul

This is where Lord Krishna, Balram, and Sudama studied under Guru Sandipani.
The place is full of mythological charm and calmness.

🕰️ Timings: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM
📍 Location: Near Mangalnath Road
💸 Entry: Free

🎯 Tip: Don’t miss the ancient Gomti Kund inside the ashram.

🌿 7. Mahakal Corridor – The New Marvel!

The newly built grand corridor around Mahakaleshwar Temple is a MUST-SEE!
Massive Shiva sculptures, murals, fountains — everything feels majestic! 💫

🕰️ Best Time: Evening (beautiful lighting!)
📍 Location: Around Mahakaleshwar Temple
💸 Entry: Free

🎯 Tip: Perfect for photos and slow evening walks. 📸
