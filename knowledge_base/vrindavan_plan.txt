Title: My Vrindavan Plan: Divine Bliss + Pure Vibes! 🙏✨

Radhe Radhe! 🌼💛
Vrindavan — the land of Radha-Krishna, where every street, every breeze, every chant feels like pure devotion.
Yahan aate hi dil halka ho jaata hai — bas shanti, pyaar, aur bhakti. 🕉️💖

Let’s explore Vrindavan in full spiritual style! 🌸😇

🕌 1. Banke Bihari Temple – Heartbeat of Vrindavan

The most vibrant and soulful temple of Vrindavan!
Here, Krishna is worshipped as Bihari Ji — playful, innocent, charming.
Darshan happens in glimpses because His energy is SO powerful! 🔥💛

🕰️ Timings:

Morning: 7:45 AM – 12 PM

Evening: 5:30 PM – 9:30 PM (varies by season)

💸 Entry: Free
📍 Location: Banke Bihari Mandir Road

🎯 Tips:

Go early — it gets extremely crowded.

Don’t miss the stunning flower decorations during Holi! 🌸🌼

🕌 2. Prem Mandir – Marble Magic ✨

The most photogenic temple of Vrindavan!
Made entirely of white marble, glowing with changing lights at night — pure heaven! 😍

🕰️ Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:30 PM
💸 Entry: Free
📍 Location: Chhatikara Road

⭐ Evening Light Show: 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM (must-watch!)

🎯 Tip: Best time to visit is after sunset — the lighting looks unreal! 🌃

🕉️ 3. ISKCON Temple – Krishna Balaram Mandir

Soulful kirtans, peaceful vibes, white marble floors —
ISKCON is where you’ll feel divine energy as soon as you enter. 🎶🙏

🕰️ Timings: 4:30 AM – 9:00 PM
💸 Entry: Free
📍 Location: Raman Reti

⭐ Don’t Miss: Evening Sandhya Aarti — the chanting and music are unforgettable! 🎵🌸

🌸 4. Nidhivan – The Mystical Forest

This is where Krishna & Radha are believed to perform Raas Leela every night.
Trees here are bent like they’re dancing! 🌿✨

⛔ Important:
No one stays after sunset — it’s strictly closed at night due to local beliefs.

🕰️ Timings: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM
📍 Location: Near Seva Kunj

🎯 Tip: Go with a guide to understand the legends!

🌺 5. Seva Kunj & Rang Mahal

Where Radha-Krishna performed their pastimes.
The artwork, the gardens, the silence — everything feels divine. 🌼💖

🕰️ Timings: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM
📍 Location: Near Nidhivan

🎯 Tip: Visit early morning for peaceful vibes.

🌅 6. Yamuna Ghat – Keshi Ghat

Keshi Ghat is Vrindavan’s most iconic riverside spot.
Aarti at sunset + boat ride = bliss! 🌅🛶✨

💸 Boat Ride: ₹50–₹150
🕰️ Best Time: Sunset

🎯 Tip: Perfect spot for photos! 📸
